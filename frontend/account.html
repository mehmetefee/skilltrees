Sign-in methods

The ways you can get back into this account. Keep at least one.

    Passkeys

    Sign in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a password. A passkey only works on this site, so it can't be phished or reused elsewhere.

      No passkeys yet.

      Password

      Change the password you sign in with. Every other browser signed in to this account will be signed out.

      At least 8 characters; a long passphrase is best, and no symbols or numbers are required. Very common passwords, and ones containing your username, are refused.

      Where you’re signed in

      Every browser signed in to this account. If you don’t recognise one, end it, then change your password.

        Your data

        Everything this site holds about your account — your trees, the sign-ins connected to it and where it is signed in — as one JSON file. Each tree in it can be imported again.

        Delete account

        Deleting your account also deletes every tree it made, for everyone, straight away. It can’t be undone.

        Want to keep your trees? Download your data first: each tree in it can be imported again, here or anywhere that reads the format.
